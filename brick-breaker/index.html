How to play

Bounce the ball off your paddle to break every brick. Some bricks drop a coin. Catch it with the paddle for a power-up.

  • MoveDrag anywhere on a phone. Mouse or arrow keys on a computer.
  • LaunchTap, click or press Space to send the ball off the paddle.
  • AimWhere the ball lands on the paddle sets its angle. The edges send it out wide.
  • ChainBreak bricks without the ball touching the paddle. Every four bricks raises the multiplier.
  • LivesYou start with three. Lose every ball and you lose a life.
