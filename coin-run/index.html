How to play

Shoot what comes at you, grab the coins it drops, and beat the boss drone at the end of each level.

  • MoveDrag on a phone. Mouse, arrow keys or WASD on a computer.
  • FireAutomatic. You can switch that off in Settings and fire with Space or a held click.
  • Doux chargeSix Naira fill it. Tap it, or press B, to clear the screen.
  • GhostTap Ghost, or press Shift, and nothing can hit you while the ship is see-through. It takes four seconds to recharge.
  • HullYou have three hull points. Every hit also costs one firepower level.
  • RocksTake a few hits. Big ones split in two.
  • DronesHover and fire at you, then dive. Heavy ones fire three shots at once, and small ones come in swarms.
  • Gold runnerStreaks across the top. Catch it for a Bitcoin.
  • Boss droneOne at the end of every level, each with its own attacks. Shots that hit its glowing eye do double damage.
